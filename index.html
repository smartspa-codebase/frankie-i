01 About

Profile

Mechanical engineer by training. Systems engineer by practice. Toolmaker by instinct.

I grew up taking things apart and I never really stopped. What changed is what I do afterward — somewhere between Madison and Munich I figured out that the interesting work isn't fixing the thing in front of you, it's building the thing that fixes all of them.

Same instinct every time: go one level up, build the leverage, hand it to everybody else. That's the whole thing.

05 Work

Problem

Hot tub chemistry is a chore people either do badly or pay someone else to do. Every wrong dose costs money, wastes chemicals, and shortens the life of the equipment.

What I built

An autonomous chemical maintenance system — sensing, dosing, and control in one unit — designed, prototyped, and integrated on a $1,000 budget. Power, mechanical dispensing, chemical sensors, fluid flow, embedded software, and the user interface, all made to work as one product rather than five subsystems in a box.

My role

Designer and project manager. I ran system design, requirement testing, and subsystem integration, and ran the team lean-agile so we could iterate fast enough to actually finish. First place out of sixty teams.
